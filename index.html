Our story

Built on six decades of trust.

"Galen House was established to provide accessible care for minor surgical procedures and common ailments, helping patients avoid the inconvenience and additional burden of travelling to larger healthcare institutions. Over the years, it evolved to provide mentorship for doctors who are now making significant contributions to the provision of health services with different levels of disciplines to the community of Bulawayo as well as development of private healthcare practice."
1950s

Dr J G M Ferguson, a family physician originally from Scotland, opens his practice in Bulawayo, the beginning of Galen House.

1980s

Dr Ferguson joins native doctors to form a united Zimbabwe Medical Association, co-establishes examinations for doctors entering private practice and continuing with Galen House

Today

Galen House is housed with facilities that offer imaging, laboratory, different specialist disciplines, physiotherapy, and ambulance services. The practice is still guided by the same founding purpose to provide easy access to health services to the Bulawayo community round the clock.

Breast Cancer Awareness · October 2026

5 steps for breast self-examination

A monthly self-check helps you learn what's normal for your body, so you notice changes early. It takes about 10 minutes.

When to checkOnce a month, 3–5 days after your period ends, when breasts are least tender.
No periods?Pick the same date each month (e.g. the 1st) if you're post-menopausal or pregnant.
See a doctor if you notice
  • A new lump or thickening in the breast or armpit
  • A change in size or shape of one breast
  • Dimpling or puckering of the skin
  • A nipple that turns inward or changes position
  • Redness, scaling or a rash on the nipple or skin
  • Fluid or blood from the nipple (not breast milk)
  • Swelling in the armpit or near the collarbone
  • Pain in one area that doesn't go away

Most breast changes are not cancer — but every change deserves a check. Don't wait for your next monthly exam.
