Our story

Built on six decades of trust.

"Galen House was set up for minor surgeries and ailments — to save people the stress of going all the way to bigger institutions. Over the years it became a training ground for the doctors who shaped private practice in Zimbabwe."
1957

Dr Garry Ferguson, a family physician originally from Scotland, opens his practice in Bulawayo — the beginning of Galen House.

1980s

Dr Ferguson helps form a united Zimbabwe Medical Association and co-establishes examinations for doctors entering private practice.

Today

Galen House has grown into a full medical centre — imaging, laboratory, specialist clinics, physiotherapy and overnight admissions — still guided by the same founding purpose.

Our people

A team built on decades of mentorship.

Founder Dr Garry Ferguson practised at Galen House for 61 years, training generations of Zimbabwean doctors before his retirement. That same spirit of mentorship and hands-on care carries through today's team of physicians, nurses, physiotherapists and support staff.
