Our story

Built on six decades of trust.

"Galen House was set up for minor surgeries and ailments, to save people the stress of going all the way to bigger institutions. Over the years it became a training ground for the doctors who shaped private practice in Zimbabwe."
1957

Dr J G M Ferguson, a family physician originally from Scotland, opens his practice in Bulawayo, the beginning of Galen House.

1980s

Dr Ferguson helps form a united Zimbabwe Medical Association and co-establishes examinations for doctors entering private practice.

Today

Galen House is housed with facilities that offer imaging, laboratory, different specialist disciplines, physiotherapy, and ambulance services. The practice is still guided by the same founding purpose to provide easy access to health services to the Bulawayo community round the clock.
